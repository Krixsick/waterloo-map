Dining at Grebel

Each meal at Grebel is crafted with quality and care by the Food Services Team who are passionate about food. Whether you are a conference guest, workshop attendee, Waterloo employee, or visiting friend or parent of a current student, you are welcome to join us for a meal and taste for yourself why people love Grebel food.

Payment

The Grebel kitchen accepts debit or credit (sorry, no cash or Watcard) 

Upon request, the front office sells all-you-can-eat meal tickets: breakfast $9.79, lunch $18.69, supper $20.89 + hst

How eating at Grebel works

Meals are self-serve and, if not using a meal ticket, items will be priced individually. Upon entering the servery you will notice item prices posted. We suggest that you grab a tray and proceed through the servery line which starts with entrees, then desserts, beverages, and delicious salad bar in the centre. Payment will be at the end of the servery line. Enjoy your meal at one of our round tables in a bright and modern dining hall. To the left of the servery you will see a door. This is where you bring your dishes for sorting.
